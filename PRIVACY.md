# Snatch Privacy Policy
Last updated: 11 November 2026

Snatch is an offline-first arcade game. No account, email, password, phone number, or social login is required.

## Information stored on your device
Your optional display name and optional age, game progress, settings, hidden leaderboard names, and a random installation identifier are stored locally on your device. Neither field is required to play. Age is used only for the local player profile and is never sent to the leaderboard.

## Online leaderboard
If you choose to submit a score, Snatch sends your display name, score, run duration, game date, and a random installation identifier to Supabase, our database provider. The public leaderboard displays display names and scores. Do not use your real full name or share personal information in your display name.

## Community safety
Display names are checked against common English and Russian profanity dictionaries before saving or submitting. You can report an inappropriate name through the Snatch support email or block that player locally on your device. Reports can be sent privately by email. Include only the leaderboard display name and relevant public score details; never include passwords or sensitive personal information.

## Permissions, tracking and cookies
Snatch does not require location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photo-library access. The current app does not use advertising trackers or third-party advertising cookies. The mobile app does not use browser cookies for gameplay.

## Retention and deletion
Your local profile can be cleared in settings. The reset-all control clears profile and game progress stored on this device. Public leaderboard entries are separate from local data and are not deleted by a local reset. To request removal of an online score, use Snatch support and provide only the public display name and approximate score/date; do not include private information. Requests are reviewed manually.

## Providers and children
Leaderboard data is hosted by Supabase. See https://supabase.com/privacy. Snatch is intended for a general audience. Do not enter a real full name or other identifying information. Parents or guardians can contact the developer through the support email.
