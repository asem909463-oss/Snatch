# Snatch Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 10 November 2026**

Snatch is an offline-first arcade game provided by Turzam LLC. This policy explains what the app stores and what is sent to the Snatch leaderboard.

## No accounts or sign-up

Snatch does not offer account registration, email sign-in, passwords, social login, or user accounts.

## Information stored on your device

Your display name and age (if you choose to enter them) are stored locally on your device. Your game progress, settings, achievements, and run history are also stored locally. Your age is not sent to our servers.

## Information sent to the leaderboard

If you play while online, Snatch may send:
- Your chosen display name (up to 18 characters)
- Your score and run duration
- The date of the run
- A randomly generated app-specific player identifier used to associate your scores and let you request deletion

Your age, contacts, precise location, email address, phone number, and real-world identity are not requested by Snatch or sent to the leaderboard. Leaderboard display names and scores are visible to other players. Please do not use your full legal name or other sensitive information as your display name.

## Service providers

Leaderboard data is hosted by Supabase in its Sydney, Australia region. Supabase processes and stores this information to provide the online leaderboard. The app uses the public Supabase publishable key; database access is restricted by Row Level Security policies.

## Cookies, advertising and analytics

The native Snatch app does not use browser cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party advertising SDKs. It does not currently include third-party analytics. The Cookie Policy explains this in more detail.

## Data retention and deletion

Local profile details and game progress remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. You can request deletion of leaderboard entries associated with this app installation from Settings. Reinstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove the local identifier needed to make that request, so use the in-app deletion option before clearing app data.

## Children's privacy

Snatch does not require an age or collect age on its servers. Do not enter personal or identifying information in your display name. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted personal information, they can use the in-app data deletion control on the device used to play.

## Security

We use database access controls and reasonable safeguards. Public leaderboards are inherently visible to other players, and no online service can guarantee absolute security or prevent all cheating or misuse.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the Snatch App Store or Google Play listing once published.

**Important:** This document is a product privacy disclosure, not legal advice. The developer should review it against the final app build, actual data flows, and applicable laws before release.
